## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a vibrant, joyful, and deeply reassuring atmosphere crafted for a premier children's celebration center in Huelva, Spain. The core objective is twofold: ignite instant happiness and anticipation in children, while projecting absolute safety, cleanliness, and effortless booking logistics to busy parents.

The design movement combines **Modern Playful Tactility** with **Warm Mediterranean Clarity**. It balances enthusiastic celebratory energy—evoked through chromatic accents, soft geometry, and organic confetti micro-motifs—with the structured legibility and airiness of an elevated service platform. The interface avoids overwhelming visual noise or childish disorder; instead, it adopts an organized, sun-drenched resort feel where parents feel guided, relieved, and confident from the first glance to the final booking confirmation.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from the radiant Atlantic light of Huelva, coastal seas, and festive confectionery, grounded in stable, ultra-clean neutral surfaces.

- **Primary (`#0096C7`, with deep sea `#023E8A` for high-contrast text and bright turquoise `#48CAE4` for dynamic states):** Communicates safety, clarity, refreshing space, and operational reliability.
- **Secondary (`#FF5A5F`, paired with petal coral `#FF758F`):** Brings exuberant warmth, celebration, and energetic calls-to-action that stand out immediately against light backgrounds.
- **Tertiary (`#FFB703` & `#FFD166`):** Sunny gold accents used for highlights, star ratings, joyful notification pips, and package callouts.
- **Accent Purple (`#7B2CBF` / `#9D4EDD`):** A touch of imaginative magic, reserved for special event badges, VIP tiers, and interactive assistant moments.
- **Canvas & Neutrals:** Backgrounds use warm ivory-white (`#FFFDF9`) and soft cream (`#F8F9FA`) to maintain warmth without muddiness. Dark neutral (`#2B2D42`) replaces harsh pitch black to ensure high accessibility (WCAG AAA) with a friendly, softened visual weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples **Plus Jakarta Sans** for expressive, joyful structural presence with **Nunito Sans** for comfortable reading density.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings rounded geometry, open counters, and gentle terminals that evoke celebration without drifting into unreadable novelty or infantile type. Used for all headings, price tags, badges, and primary interactive buttons.
- **Nunito Sans** is calibrated specifically for parents reviewing safety guidelines, nutritional information, allergen alerts, and package itemizations. Its humanist foundation ensures effortless scanning under varied lighting conditions on mobile devices.
- Letter spacing is intentionally pulled tighter on extra-bold hero display sizes to establish a cohesive, poster-like impact, and expanded slightly on small labels and badges for crisp legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt-based fluid responsive grid system designed to adapt cleanly between on-the-go smartphone checks and detailed home tablet/desktop planning:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with max-width container capped at 1280px. Gutters set to `1.5rem` and outer page margins to `3rem`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` safe side padding.

Spacing is deliberately generous around high-cognitive sections (such as package tiers and scheduling modules) to avoid decision fatigue. Multi-column pricing tables automatically reflow into unified swipeable cards on screens below 768px, keeping all pricing variables stacked and transparent.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **multi-layered soft ambient shadows tinted with brand primaries** combined with crisp hairline borders, keeping elements feeling light, buoyant, and tactile rather than heavy.

- **Flat/Ground:** Canvas `#FFFDF9` and card inset surfaces `#F4F6F8` for unselected options and input backdrops.
- **Elevation 1 (Cards, Inputs, Base Components):** `0px 4px 16px -2px rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.05)`, bordered by a 1px solid rim (`rgba(0, 150, 199, 0.08)`).
- **Elevation 2 (Interactive Hover, Highlighted Package Cards):** `0px 12px 28px -4px rgba(0, 150, 199, 0.12), 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(255, 90, 95, 0.08)`.
- **Elevation 3 (Sticky Headers, Floating Action Badges, Modals):** `0px 20px 40px -8px rgba(43, 45, 66, 0.14)`. Backdrop filters use `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with 85% opacity surfaces on sticky navigation bars.
- **Playful Micro-depth:** Key buttons utilize a subtle 2px pseudo-3D offset bottom shade to provide an inviting, pressable "cushion" feeling without cluttering the interface.

## Shapes

The design system embraces high-curvature, friendly organic geometry (`roundedness: 3`).

- **Buttons & Pills:** Full pill contour (`9999px`) across all interactive triggers, status indicators, and filter tags.
- **Cards & Containers:** `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px) for interactive calculator modules and feature callouts, stepping up to `rounded-3xl` (2rem / 32px) for hero containers, pricing cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Confetti Micro-Shapes:** Subtle circular confetti dots (6px to 14px), rounded starlets, and soft pill-shaped confetti sprinkles sprinkled in low-contrast tint backgrounds to build celebratory texture without competing with key copy.

## Components

### 1. Sticky Navigation & Floating CTAs
- **Sticky Header:** Frosted warm-white glass (`rgba(255, 253, 249, 0.9)`) housing the celebratory logo, quick links, direct phone link, and an attention-grabbing Coral button (`Reservar Ahora`).
- **Floating Quick-Action Dock (Mobile/Desktop):** Pinned to bottom right with direct WhatsApp trigger (`#25D366` pulse ring) and instant quick-call button with active status pill ("¡Respondemos al momento!").

### 2. Buttons & Badges
- **Primary Action (Coral Pink):** Pill-shaped, gradient from `#FF5A5F` to `#FF758F`, bold white label, slight tactile drop (`active: translate-y-0.5`).
- **Secondary Action (Sea Blue):** Pill-shaped `#0096C7` with white text for utility actions.
- **Package Badges:** Upper-card overflow ribbon with `'Más Popular'` in tertiary gold (`#FFB703`) with dark navy typography and a miniature star icon.

### 3. Interactive Birthday Budget Calculator
- Smooth range slider for child count (e.g., 8 to 35 niños) with dynamic real-time price updates.
- Checkbox pills for add-ons (Tarta temática, Animación infantil, Menú para padres, Piñata artesanal).
- Sticky bottom summary tray revealing estimated per-child cost and a single-click button to export the breakdown directly to WhatsApp.

### 4. Package Pricing Comparison Cards
- Three-tier card architecture (Básico, Súper Fiesta, Experiencia Ocio VIP).
- Center card ("Más Popular") scaled up by 4% with a glowing coral boundary border and pre-checked amenities list.
- Clear checklist items with bi-color icons: bright turquoise checkmarks for included items, soft grey dashes for exclusions.

### 5. Form Fields, Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Inputs use rounded-xl boundaries, clean 1.5px border transitions to `#0096C7`, and soft cream background tint.
- Checkboxes and radio buttons feature generous hit zones (minimum 44x44px target area) with rounded-full check-rings, ideal for thumb tapping on mobile screens.

### 6. Interactive Assistant Modal & FAQs
- **Mini Bot Assistant:** Floating chat modal dressed with friendly host avatar, quick pre-set questions ("¿Qué incluye la merienda?", "¿Tienen opciones sin gluten?", "¿Puedo llevar mi propia tarta?").
- **Accordion FAQs:** Pill-bordered independent cards with spring animation on expand/collapse and bold question labels.
